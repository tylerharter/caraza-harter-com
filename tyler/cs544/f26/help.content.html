CS 544 Help

Office Hours

How:

  • check the Office Hour Schedule to see your options
  • just walk in or join Zoom at an available time

Best for:

  • debugging project code and error messages
  • conceptual questions that are faster to work through interactively
  • anything where it helps to have somebody look at your screen

Policies:

  • office hours are "walk in", no need to schedule an appointment

Piazza

How:

  • post your question on Piazza

Best for:

  • questions of general interest to the class
  • clarifications about a project spec or lecture
  • course logistics

Policies:

  • do not post project code snippets that are >5 lines long, that's considered cheating
  • private questions are not enabled -- use the Help Request Form instead

TA Email

How:

  • general question: fill out the Help Request Form; a TA will follow up with you over email, and you can reply on that thread
  • grading: email the TA who graded you directly
  • office hour follow-up: to follow up with somebody specific after office hours, look up the teaching team by face here

Best for:

  • individual questions about your own situation
  • grading questions

Policies:

  • if you're working with a partner, keep them CC'd on the thread
  • if a TA hasn't responded to the initial form or followup messages within 2 business days, CC the instructor on the same thread
  • don't start multiple threads with different people on the same topic

Instructor

How:

  • email me directly at tharter@wisc.edu
  • for an appointment outside my office hours, email me about availability

Best for:

  • you have a conceptual question about lecture
  • you're building something cool and want to chat about it
  • you feel you're having trouble keeping up with the course
  • you have feedback about the course or a TA

Policies:

  • I shouldn't be your first point of contact for project troubleshooting or grading concerns (it doesn't scale to a class this size)
  • feel free to escalate anything to me after giving the TAs a fair chance to help you

Campus Help

Extra Support/Accommodations

As a student, you may experience a range of issues that can be barriers to learning. These might include isolation, difficult financial situations, strained relationships, anxiety, high levels of stress, alcohol/drug problems, feeling down, loss of loved one, and/or loss of motivation. Services exist on campus to support students who find themselves in these situations, like University Health Services and the Dean of Students Office. You can learn more about free, confidential mental health services at UHS by calling 608-265-5600 Opt. 2 or visiting uhs.wisc.edu. Drop-in staff are available daily at the Office of Student Assistance and Support (OSAS) to support students and answer questions. To learn more about OSAS, please call 608-263-5700 or visit osas.wisc.edu.

In general, if you have any issues keeping up with the course for any reason, it's better to let me know -- I'll do my best to connect you with resources and try to think of accommodations to help out. I want to do whatever I can to help you succeed.

Student Grievance Procedure

Any student at UW–Madison who feels that they have been treated unfairly has the right to voice a complaint and receive a prompt hearing of the grievance. The basis for a grievance can range from something as subtle as miscommunication to the extreme of harassment.

Ensuring that all students feel welcome and supported is a top priority of our department. The Department of Computer Sciences has developed procedures to handle complaints or incidents that are student-related, such as bias or mistreatment, or academic, such as a grade dispute or incident with an instructor. Information on the CS reporting procedures can be found here: https://www.cs.wisc.edu/report-an-incident/.

For assistance in determining options, students can also contact the drop-in staff member with OSAS (see above).
